Episode 01

Tula Bischof

Licensed Psychologist & Founder, Loving To Love

Tula is a KCPB-registered Decolonial psychologist based in Nairobi whose practice, Loving To Love, is built around decolonial and liberation-oriented work. She holds space for the systems, cultures, and relationships that shape people's lives, especially the ones that often get overlooked. For episode one, she turned the mic on Kiarie and got him talking about the breakdown, the silence before it, and why he started this show.

Episode 02

Keith Nyambane

Leadership Coach & Founder, StaRise Mentors Africa

Keith is the founder of StaRise Mentors Africa and has spent more than a decade mentoring boys and young men in leadership and personal growth. In this episode, he joins Kiarie for an honest conversation about the state of the Kenyan man, exploring mentorship, masculinity, emotional health, relationships, responsibility, and why lasting growth comes from embracing the process rather than chasing quick success.
